CONCORDE

The Concorde was the world's first Supersonic design ever. It was designed by the British BOAC and the French Aerospatiale.However, it stopped flying in other countries because its sonic booms were too loud.

BOEING 2707

The Americans worried that Britain and France would take over the aviation market with the Concorde. Their answer to this problem was the Boeing 2707, which was faster and had a higher passenger capacity.
Sadly, it never really left the drawing board.

TUPOLEV TU144

The Soviets were not lagging behind either. Their tu-144 was slightly faster than the Concorde. Unlike the 2707, this aircraft did make it into service.
It travelled on only one route for 8 months and never entered service ever again.
