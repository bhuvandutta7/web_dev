Madam Marie Curie

A Mini Biography

Marie Curie (1867–1934) was a pioneering Polish-French physicist and chemist who revolutionized science.

She remains the first woman to win a Nobel Prize and the only person in history to win in two different scientific fields: Physics (1903) and Chemistry (1911).

The first element discovered by Madam Marie Curie and her husband Pierre Curie was Radium Polonium

Its chemical formula is 209 Po 84.
